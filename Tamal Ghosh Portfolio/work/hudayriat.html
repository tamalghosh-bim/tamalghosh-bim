Hudayriat Island: dry-utility corridors

Modelling the power and telecom networks for a large island development in Abu Dhabi, and changing how the team produces its drawings.

01Context

The project

Hudayriat Island is a large mixed-use development off the coast of Abu Dhabi. Its roads carry dry utilities (power, telecom and street lighting) in shared corridors that have to fit around the road geometry, the wet utilities and the finished levels.

02Role

What I was responsible for

  • Model the dry-utility networks in 3D, in step with the road design.
  • Prepare drawings from the models for review and construction.
  • Train and support the modelling team.

03Challenge

The problem to solve

Drawings were prepared with XREFs: every sheet referenced whole drawing files. Files were heavy, references broke when folders moved, and a change in one model had to be chased through every sheet that used it.

04Approach

How I approached it

Utilities as corridors

Built the dry-utility runs as Civil 3D corridors that follow the road alignment, so offsets and levels update when the road design changes.

Data shortcuts instead of XREFs

Moved drawing production to data shortcuts. Each model is referenced once, and each sheet pulls in only the objects it needs.

Scripts for the repetitive work

Wrote AutoLISP routines and Dynamo scripts with the help of AI for labelling, checks and sheet set-up, and tested each one before the team used it.

Training the team

Trained the modellers in the new workflow and wrote it down, so the team keeps working the same way.

05Outcome

What it achieved

XREF → DSDrawing production moved to data shortcuts
3DDry-utility networks modelled as corridors
TeamModellers trained in the new workflow
