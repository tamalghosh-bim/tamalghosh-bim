01Context

The project

Pinnacle Infotech delivers BIM services to clients around the world from its offices in India. Here Tamal worked on infrastructure packages for clients in Singapore and Taiwan, as well as on utility, airport, defence and building projects.

02Role

What I was responsible for

  • Civil 3D and Revit model development.
  • Navisworks clash coordination and BIM QA/QC.
  • Preparing BIM Execution Plans (BEP), Master Information Delivery Plans (MIDP) and responses to Employer’s Information Requirements (EIR).
  • Coordinating with client BIM managers, designers and engineers.

03Challenge

The problem to solve

Working remotely means the model is the only common ground. It has to be complete, consistent and easy to check by people who will never meet the modelling team.

04Approach

How I approached it

Agree the information requirements

Started from the client’s EIR, and wrote the BEP and MIDP that set out what would be delivered, when, and to what standard.

Model to the standard

Built Civil 3D and Revit models that follow the agreed naming, levels of detail and structure.

Federate and clash

Combined the discipline models in Navisworks and resolved clashes with the client’s designers.

Check before issue

Ran BIM QA/QC on every delivery, so models arrived ready to use.
