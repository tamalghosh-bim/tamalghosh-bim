01Context

The project

King Salman Park is a major urban park project in Riyadh. After the KSAB project, the client approached Pinnacle Infotech to have Tamal back on site for this one.

02Role

What I was responsible for

  • BIM coordination and modelling for structure, architecture and MEP.
  • Shop drawings, BOQ and BBS from the models.
  • Lead the site modelling team (five modellers).
  • Point of contact for the Pinnacle site team, including invoicing and day-to-day management.

03Challenge

The problem to solve

The site needed coordinated shop drawings, quantities and bar-bending schedules from several disciplines at once, and the BIM team on site started as one person.

04Approach

How I approached it

Build the site team

Grew the on-site BIM team from one person to fifteen, and set up how work was split between site and the offshore office.

Coordinate the disciplines

Helped the structure, architecture and MEP teams with modelling, and resolved clashes before drawings were issued.

Quantities from the model

Produced BOQs and bar-bending schedules from the models, so quantities matched the drawings.

Run the commercial side

Handled invoicing and management work as the site point of contact.
