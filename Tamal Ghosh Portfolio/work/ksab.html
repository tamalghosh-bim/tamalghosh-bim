01Context

The project

Tamal’s first on-site assignment in Riyadh, deputed from Pinnacle Infotech. Shop drawings were produced by an offshore team, then built by site teams working from survey data.

02Role

What I was responsible for

  • Coordinate with the construction and survey teams.
  • Update the BIM model and design to match site conditions.
  • Extract BOQs and prepare site clarification requests (SCRs).
  • Coordinate architecture, structure, mechanical and other trades, on site and offsite.

03Challenge

The problem to solve

Drawings prepared offsite did not always match what was on the ground. Every mismatch meant a rejected drawing, a query and lost time on site.

04Approach

How I approached it

Listen to the site first

Met the survey team and site engineers to understand what was slowing them down.

Feed it back to the drawing office

Briefed the offsite team so shop drawings were made for real site conditions.

Keep the model current

Updated the model and design whenever site conditions changed.

Raise clarifications early

Prepared more than 50 SCRs, so questions were answered before they held up work.

05Outcome

What it achieved

300+Shop drawings approved by the consultant in two months
200+Man-hours saved on site
$74.7KPending payment recovered from the client

The design manager named Tamal best coordinator, and he became the first choice of BIM coordinator for the next project, King Salman Park.
